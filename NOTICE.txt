Population models beside a census
=================================

This repository's own files are CC0 1.0 (LICENSE). The data it carries and the data it reads keep their own terms.

Census, Botswana: Statistics Botswana, Population and Housing Census 2022: Population Distribution, Structure and
          Density in Botswana, March 2024, Table 1. Transcribed by this project into
          data/botswana_census_2022_districts.csv, with each district's name in the boundary file added beside it.
          Terms of use (statsbots.org.bw/terms-use): the information may be used provided Statistics Botswana is
          acknowledged as the source of the basic data, and any analysis is stated to result from the user's own
          processing. The comparison here is this project's own processing of the data.

Census, Rwanda: National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR), Fifth Rwanda Population and Housing Census 2022,
          Main Indicators, Table 1 (PHC5-2022_Main_Indicators.xlsx, 581,274 bytes, updated 4 February 2025). The 30
          district counts are in data/rwanda_census_2022_districts.csv; the workbook is not redistributed. NO TERMS OF
          USE could be read: NISR's site returned server errors on 2026-11-13. Used, credited to NISR, by the
          practitioner's decision of that day; to be revisited if NISR's terms say otherwise.

Boundaries, Botswana: geoBoundaries gbHumanitarian ADM2, release 9469f09, from UNFPA via HDX, 2021, CC BY 3.0 IGO.
          28 units, one per census district. Carried inside the project.

Boundaries, Rwanda: geoBoundaries gbOpen ADM2, release 9469f09, from Open Data Rwanda, 2012, CC BY 4.0. 30 units.
          Carried inside the project.

WorldPop: Bondarenko M. et al. 2025. Constrained estimates of 2015-2030 total number of people per grid square,
          R2025A version v1. WorldPop, University of Southampton. doi:10.5258/SOTON/WP00839. CC BY 4.0.

GRID3:    Gadiaga A. et al. 2025. Gridded 2025 population estimates for sub-Saharan Africa constrained to GRID3
          settlement extents. WorldPop, University of Southampton. doi:10.5258/SOTON/WP00851. CC BY 4.0.

GHS-POP:  Schiavina M., Freire S., Carioli A., MacManus K. (2023): GHS-POP R2023A, GHS population grid multitemporal
          (1975-2030). European Commission, Joint Research Centre. doi:10.2905/2FF68A52-5B5B-4A22-8F40-C41DA8332CFE.
          CC BY 4.0.

None of the rasters is carried here: each is read where it is published, or from a file downloaded from there.

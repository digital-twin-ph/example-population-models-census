# Population models beside a census

A Fieldwork worked example. Three gridded population estimates, WorldPop, GRID3 and GHS-POP, are each totalled over a
country's administrative units with **Sample a raster at points or over areas**, and set beside the census count for the
same units.

The country is chosen by one rule: a recent census whose reference year is close to the years the three products
offer, published per administrative unit under an open licence, on boundaries that match a published boundary set.

Not yet populated: the country, its census table, its boundaries, the workflows and the manual are added once the
census is chosen. Each file's source and licence will be stated in `provenance.json` and `NOTICE.txt`.
